License CC BY 4.0. PubChem and ClinicalTrials.gov remain the authoritative upstream records; these files are a reduced extract.

compounds.json

111 catalog entries. Slug, name, aliases, categories, formula, molecular weight, sequence, CAS, PubChem CID, UniProt, approval flag, and synthesis features. Narrative monographs stay on the .com.

compounds.csv

The same table. List fields are joined with a vertical bar.

pk-half-lives.json

44 half-life rows the pharmacokinetic helpers read, including nulls where no human figure is stored, plus the source note beside each row.

trials-index.json

25-study snapshot from the ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, five studies for each of five query terms, retrieved 18 November 2026 (America/Chicago). Status fields go stale; re-query upstream before citing one.

references.json

Structured labels, PMIDs, and registries named by the methods pages and the half-life notes.
