Changelog

What changed, in public

Spec versions are frozen at their URLs. This log is append-only. Corrections to a published spec are also recorded under errata and do not rewrite the old page.

2026-11-18 — site opens

  • Published Evidence Tier Schema v0.1, frozen to patch 0.1.1, with JSON Schema. Patch pins: 0.1.1 and archived 0.1.0.
  • Recorded erratum E-001: the 0.1.0 freshness table mixed an estimate kind into the tier column. 0.1.1 states that the kind override wins and commercial is 30 days.
  • Published methods for reconstitution, blends, and pharmacokinetics, matching the reference modules.
  • Released dataset snapshot 2026-11-18: compound identity (111 rows), half-life inputs (44 rows), trials index (25 studies), and structured references. License CC BY 4.0.
