## Generation prompt

Create a professional 4-column by 3-row contact sheet of flat vector editorial
illustrations for American Peptide Methods, a public scientific standards
website. Exactly 12 equally sized rectangular tiles, each tile an independent
illustration, crisp pale ivory background with generous margins, no gutters
needed, tile boundaries on exact grid. Limited ink navy, muted teal and golden
ochre. Absolutely no text, lettering, digits, ticks, badges, syringes or medical
imagery. Top row left to right: an abstract document with one claim highlighted;
a stack of source documents under a magnifier; a three-tier abstract stepped
classification diagram; a citation represented by linked quotation shapes.
Middle row: two lab volumes combining in a transparent unmarked laboratory
vessel; two differently colored lab sample streams merging into an unmarked test
vessel; laboratory sample comparison using a balance and colored blocks; clean
exponential decay curve on unmarked coordinate axes. Bottom row: two distinct
release cover illustrations, first using foundational geometric layered
document shapes, second using same shapes with a new small gold accent and an
intersecting circular motif; last two tiles blank ivory. Avoid gradients or
textures. Each tile's objects contained comfortably within its own center for
slicing.
