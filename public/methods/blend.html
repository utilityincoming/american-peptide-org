Method · reference tier

Blend draws

Tool: https://americanpeptide.com/tools/blend-calculator · Module: packages/calculators/blend.ts

What it answers

Several peptides share one vial and one diluent volume. Each has a mass in milligrams and an intended dose in micrograms. The method reports how many shared draws the blend can make, the volume of each draw, and the micrograms of each peptide that draw actually contains.

Formulas

For each component, mass M is in mg and intended dose D is in mcg. The only unit conversion in this method is 1 mg = 1000 mcg.

standalone doses of i     S_i = (M_i × 1000) / D_i
draws in the blend         N = floor(min S_i)     over valid rows
draw volume                W / N
U-100 units                (W / N) × 100
delivered mcg of i         (M_i × 1000) / N
over fraction              (delivered − D_i) / D_i

Worked examples

5 mg at 250 mcg and 10 mg at 500 mcg, in 2 mL. Each component supports 20 doses. The blend makes 20 draws of 0.1 mL (10 units). Delivered matches intended, so the over fraction is 0.

5 mg at 250 mcg and 5 mg at 500 mcg. Standalone counts are 20 and 10. The blend makes 10 draws. The second peptide delivers 500 mcg. The first delivers 500 mcg against an intent of 250 mcg, an over fraction of 1. The website highlights an over fraction above 0.02. That threshold is a display rule, not part of the math.

Assumptions

  • Every peptide is transferred in full into one vial. Losses on the spatula, the syringe, or the wall are ignored.
  • One draw volume serves every peptide. You cannot hit two intended doses with one volume unless the masses are proportioned to the same dose count.
  • The diluent volume is the final volume. Displacement by the powder is ignored.
  • Components are independent solutes. The method has no stability, compatibility, or concentration-limit term.
  • A cap of ten peptides on the website is a form limit. The function takes any list length.

Edge cases

  • A row with non-positive mass or dose is skipped. If no row is valid, dose count and draw volume are 0.
  • Diluent of 0 yields a dose count from the masses and a draw volume of 0.
  • When the minimum standalone count is not an integer, floor drops the partial dose. Delivered amounts are then computed from that integer, so they step up relative to the intended dose.

Scope note

Unit arithmetic from the stated inputs. This is a value, not a dosing recommendation. Sharing a vial is a handling decision this method does not endorse: it removes a per-peptide check against a certificate of analysis, and it says nothing about whether the peptides remain intact in one solution.
